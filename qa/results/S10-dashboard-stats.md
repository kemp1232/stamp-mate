## Defects

### Defect S10-D1 — `getCustomerList` has no pagination/limit (unbounded query + unbounded render)
- **Severity:** Low (confirmed scalability/UX gap, not a functional bug — matches the test plan's own expectation, not a [SUSPECTED DEFECT] item)
- **Test case ID(s):** S10-009
- **File:line:** `src/lib/dashboard.ts:68-91` (`getCustomerList`, no `take`), rendered by `src/app/dashboard/customers/page.tsx:31-54` (maps the full array into `<li>` with no virtualization/windowing)
- **Repro:** Seed a business with 120+ customers (see `qa/S10-dashboard-stats/run.js`, S10-009 block), log in as its owner, load `/dashboard/customers`.
- **Observed vs Expected:** Observed: the page fetched and rendered all 120 rows in ~680ms with no errors — functionally correct today. Expected/at-risk: as customer counts grow into the thousands, both the Prisma query (full table scan + row-per-customer subqueries for `loyaltyCards`) and the client-side render (hundreds/thousands of `<li>` DOM nodes with no windowing) will scale linearly with no ceiling, degrading load time and potentially timing out.
- **Suggested fix:** Add cursor- or offset-based pagination to `getCustomerList` (e.g. `take`/`skip` with a page-size like 25–50) and corresponding UI controls, or switch to virtualized list rendering if "show everyone at once" is a deliberate product choice.

### Defect S10-D2 — No `loading.tsx` at the `/dashboard` root route
- **Severity:** Low
- **Test case ID(s):** S10-010
- **File:line:** missing `src/app/dashboard/loading.tsx` (present for `dashboard/customers`, `dashboard/program`, `dashboard/stats`, but not the root `dashboard/page.tsx`)
- **Repro:** `find src/app/dashboard -iname loading.tsx` — only 3 of 4 dashboard routes have one.
- **Observed vs Expected:** Observed: `/dashboard` itself (the landing page after login, which runs `getStaffMembershipsForUser` + `getDashboardStats`) has no dedicated loading skeleton; Next.js falls back to the nearest ancestor `loading.tsx` (none exists above it) or a blank transition. Expected: consistent with its sibling routes, it should have its own skeleton for a slow first paint after login.
- **Suggested fix:** Add `src/app/dashboard/loading.tsx` mirroring the pattern used in the sibling routes.
